HOW IT WORKS

A geometry-first vectorization pipeline.

ClusterGlyph keeps color segmentation, connected-object extraction, contour optimization, and editable SVG serialization explicit and inspectable.

01

Cluster color

Pixels are grouped in perceptual Lab color space, producing a controlled palette before any tracing begins.

02

Separate objects

Every spatially connected component becomes its own stable SVG object, even when multiple objects share a color.

03

Reduce points

Collinear cleanup plus Ramer–Douglas–Peucker simplification removes unnecessary polygon vertices.

04

Edit & recover geometry

Drag polygon vertices directly in the preview. Round contours can become native <circle> or <ellipse> primitives.
