HOW IT WORKS

From contour tracing to semantic reconstruction.

ClusterGlyph asks which SVG object most likely produced each raster region, rather than blindly turning every edge color into another polygon.

01

Recover typography

Source-resolution Tesseract.js groups words into lines. Candidate fonts are rendered and compared with source glyph masks, then exported with exact line-width fitting.

02

Repair the raster

Detected glyph masks are dilated to include anti-aliasing, filled with the estimated local background, and isolated cluster fringes are merged.

03

Recover primitives

Panels become rectangles, round objects become circles/ellipses, thin strokes become lines, and smooth contours can become cubic Bézier paths.

04

Group & export

Panels, artwork, and OCR blocks receive semantic groups and stable metadata before editable SVG serialization.
