HOW IT WORKS

A text-aware, geometry-first vectorization pipeline.

ClusterGlyph keeps OCR, color segmentation, connected-object extraction, contour optimization, and editable SVG serialization explicit and inspectable.

01

Recover text

Tesseract.js detects words in-browser. ClusterGlyph estimates their color, common font family, weight, size, and original placement.

02

Cluster & separate

Graphics are grouped in perceptual Lab color space and every spatially connected component becomes its own SVG object.

03

Reduce points

Collinear cleanup plus Ramer–Douglas–Peucker simplification removes unnecessary polygon vertices.

04

Export semantic SVG

Round contours become <circle>/<ellipse>; detected words become native <text> instead of traced letter polygons.
