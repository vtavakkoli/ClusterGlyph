HOW IT WORKS

From raster image to editable vector.

Start with geometry tracing. Enable experimental text recovery only when you want to try replacing traced letters with editable text.

01

Choose your image

Drop a logo, diagram, or illustration. Choose a color count and processing resolution to balance detail and SVG size.

02

Cluster & separate

Graphics are grouped in perceptual Lab color space and every spatially connected component becomes its own SVG object.

03

Reduce points

Collinear cleanup plus Ramer–Douglas–Peucker simplification removes unnecessary polygon vertices.

04

Export semantic SVG

Edit shapes and export SVG. Optional experimental OCR can recover native <text>; wording, fonts, and placement need review.
